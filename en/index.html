Where our coils go.

  • 01

    Smartphone cameras

    Autofocus and optical image stabilization actuators move the lens by microns. The coil decides how evenly they move.

  • 02

    Surveillance cameras

    Auto-tracking security cameras follow movement by driving their lens and mechanism with small coils that have to run reliably around the clock.

  • 03

    Wireless charging

    From phones and smartwatches to electric vehicles, the coil and its magnetic shield set how efficiently power crosses the gap and how warm the device gets.

  • 04

    Optical drives

    CD, VCD and DVD laser pickups focus and track the beam with tiny coils that move the objective lens.

  • 05

    Medical & hearing

    Ultrasound equipment and hearing aids need coils that are small, consistent and documented.

  • 06

    Home & personal devices

    Everyday products from robot vacuums to electric toothbrushes rely on small coils for motion, sensing and charging.

  • 07

    Radio & induction

    Radio receivers and electromagnetic induction products pick up or create fields with coils tuned for their frequency.

In short

What does this company make?
Arcwind manufactures VCM coils, air-core coils, bobbin coils, wireless charging coils, linear motors and wireless chargers.
How much can it make?
Capacity is about 20 million coils a month across all types. About 10% of voice-coil-motor (VCM) coils shipped worldwide are wound in its workshop.
What equipment does it use?
A 1,800 m² workshop with more than 90 employees, more than 50 winding machines (most of them Nittoku machines imported from Japan) and more than 30 machines for soldering, tray loading, visual inspection, automated assembly and quality testing.
Does it make custom coils?
Yes. Most parts are wound to customer drawings under OEM or ODM terms. The quality management system is certified to ISO 9001 and materials are RoHS compliant.
