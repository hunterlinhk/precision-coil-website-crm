Cutting OIS calibration time with matched coil sets

A camera-module maker was spending 40 seconds per module on OIS calibration. Matched coil sets brought it to 26.

18 Oct 20265 min readArcwind engineering team

The problem

A tier-one camera-module maker producing main cameras for a flagship phone found that OIS calibration had become their bottleneck. Coil-to-coil resistance spread forced the calibration routine to iterate.

What we changed

We introduced resistance binning after 100% electrical test and shipped coils as matched sets of four, with set spread at or below 1.5%. Trays were labelled by set so the line could load them without sorting.

Result

- Calibration time fell from 40 to 26 seconds per module. - First-pass yield at calibration rose from 96.1% to 98.9%. - The customer moved a second module programme to matched sets within the quarter.
