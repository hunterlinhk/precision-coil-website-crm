About this part

A low-profile coil for ball-guided and suspension-wire OIS actuators. Coils are binned by resistance and shipped as matched sets so all four drive axes behave the same, which shortens the module maker's calibration time.

What we can change

Set matching tolerance, embedded Hall-sensor window, FPC-bondable lead finish.
