Smartphone cameras

Autofocus, OIS and periscope actuators move lenses by microns, thousands of times a second. The coil decides how evenly they move.

What matters here

  • Window tolerance of ±0.02 mm so coils drop onto carriers
  • Matched sets for four-axis OIS
  • Volumes of tens of millions per month with flat yield

How we handle it

We wind about one in ten VCM coils shipped worldwide. Module makers send a drawing; we return first articles with a full dimensional and electrical report.
