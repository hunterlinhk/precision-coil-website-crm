Common questions

Can we sign an NDA before sending drawings?
Yes. Tick the confidentiality option on the form and we send our NDA before opening your files.
How are development costs handled?
With existing wire and bobbin materials there is usually only a fixture charge, refundable against production orders. New bobbin tooling is quoted separately.
Do you take lower-volume programmes?
Yes. Samples and small batches are wound to drawing; the minimum order depends on whether new fixtures are needed.
